Share your referral link, and when someone joins Rabbithole through it you earn a share of the rewards they go on to earn, in every campaign they deposit into from then on. Their rewards are not reduced. Your share comes out of each campaign's fee, not their earnings.

## Your Referral Link

There are two places to copy it:

- **Referrals** in the profile menu shows your link with a copy button.
- In any campaign panel, **Refer friends and earn a share of what they earn** copies a link that also opens that campaign. **Share on X** posts the same link.

Your link carries your Rabbithole username, or your wallet address if you have not claimed a username. A link shared from a campaign opens that campaign, but the referral itself is not tied to it. It applies to every campaign your referee joins afterward.

## How Attribution Works

- **First link wins, for good.** A person can have one referrer, and it applies to every campaign. If they already came in through someone else's link, yours does nothing.
- **It is applied when they sign in.** Rabbithole saves the referral when your link is opened, and applies it once they sign in with a wallet linked to their account. There is nothing for them to sign. They see **Referral applied** when it lands.
- **It counts from then on.** You earn from campaigns they first deposit into *after* the referral is applied. Campaigns they were already in before that do not count.
- **It follows the person, not the deposit.** Once a campaign counts, everything they earn in it for its whole run counts toward your share, including later top-ups.

A referral is not applied when the link is your own (including another wallet on your account), when the person you are referring already referred you, or when the link's username does not exist. In each case the app says why.

## What You Earn

You earn **25% of what your referees actually earn** in each campaign that has a referral program, so retained deposits are worth more to you than a large deposit that leaves the next day. Campaigns with a referral program show the referral link in their panel; campaigns without one pay no referral share.

Each campaign's referral pool is fixed when the campaign is created. If referrers collectively earn more than the pool, every referrer's share in that campaign is scaled down proportionally at the end.

## Referral Earnings

**Referrals** in the profile menu lists every campaign where you have referees, with what you have earned in each.

</Frame>

While a campaign is running the amount is marked **Estimated**, because both your referees' earnings and the pool split can still move. After the campaign ends and is finalized, the amount locks and a **Claim** button appears once the payout is published. Referral payouts are claimed separately from your own campaign rewards, one campaign at a time, and are paid in the campaign's reward token on its reward chain.

<Warning>
  **Each payout has a claim window.** Once it closes, unclaimed referral payouts are gone. The profile menu shows a dot when a payout is ready, and the Referrals page flags a payout on its last day to claim.
</Warning>

## For Protocols and Partners

Protocols decide at creation whether their campaign has a referral pool and how large it is, and partners with an integration can attribute referrals programmatically. See [Referrals](/campaigns/referrals) in the Campaigns section.
