Rewards you claim during each raffle week determine your tier for that week. Your tier controls your share of the weekly USDC reward pool and the multiplier used to calculate your [raffle entries](/rabbithole/raffle#how-entries-are-calculated).

## The Leaderboard

Each raffle contains four fixed, consecutive weekly periods. The weekly leaderboard starts fresh at the beginning of each period and ranks wallets by the unweighted USD value of rewards claimed during that week. It is not a rolling seven-day window.

The current week remains live until its cutoff. Claims made during that period can move your rank and tier. When the week closes, Rabbithole settles the final claimed value, rank, and tier for that period. The next raffle-aligned week then begins.

The all-time leaderboard remains a view of the USD value of rewards you have claimed since joining. It does not determine your weekly tier.

Each row shows the rank, the X handle or address, the number of campaigns the person is earning in, and the amount claimed. A check mark next to a name means a [verified account](/rabbithole/getting-started#verified-account).

## Tiers

Your tier is based on the unweighted USD value of rewards you claim during the current week:

| Tier | Weekly claimed USD |
| --- | ---: |
| Bronze | Under `$1` |
| Silver | `$1` or more |
| Gold | `$10` or more |
| Platinum | `$25` or more |
| Diamond | `$100` or more |

Everyone starts each weekly period at Bronze. Your tier can only move upward as you claim more during that week. At the weekly cutoff, your tier is finalized for that period and used for both the weekly reward calculation and that week's raffle-entry calculation.

<Note>
  Campaign multipliers do not affect your tier. Rabbithole uses your unweighted claimed USD to determine the tier, then applies campaign multipliers only when calculating raffle entries.
</Note>

## The Weekly Reward Pool

Rabbithole distributes a **$500 USDC pool each week**, for a total of **$2,000 USDC across the four-week raffle**. Silver, Gold, Platinum, and Diamond wallets share the pool. Bronze has a weight of `0` and does not receive a share.

| Tier | Reward weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Bronze | `0` |
| Silver | `100` |
| Gold | `110` |
| Platinum | `125` |
| Diamond | `150` |

The weights make each claimed dollar count more at higher tiers. They are relative weights, not fixed USDC amounts or percentage returns.

```text
wallet score = weekly claimed USD × tier weight

wallet reward =
  $500 × wallet score / sum of all eligible wallet scores
```

For example, a Gold wallet that claims `$20` has a score of `20 × 110 = 2,200`. Its final reward is `$500 × 2,200` divided by the sum of every eligible wallet's score that week. Because that total changes as other people claim, any reward shown before the cutoff is a projection.

### Settlement and Publication

<Steps>
  <Step title="The Week Closes">
    Rabbithole fixes each wallet's claimed USD, tier, score, and share of that week's pool. Final amounts are calculated to USDC precision.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rewards Are Published">
    Your weekly amount is added to your total for the four-week cycle. Rabbithole publishes the latest cumulative entitlement through a reward campaign, so each update includes all settled weeks so far.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You Claim">
    Once the latest reward data is published, the app shows the unpaid amount as claimable. You can claim during the cycle; when another week is published, you can claim the newly added balance without claiming an earlier week twice.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If no eligible wallet records a positive score in a week, no share is allocated from that week's pool.

## Raffle Entries

The weekly reward pool does not use campaign multipliers. Raffle entries do: each eligible campaign's claimed USD is weighted by its campaign multiplier, then your weekly tier multiplier is applied and the result is rounded down.

See [Raffle](/rabbithole/raffle) for the complete entry formula, public snapshot, on-chain commitment, and winner-verification steps.

## How to Reach the Next Tier

- Claim at least `$1` during the week to reach Silver and become eligible for both systems.
- Claim across any eligible Rabbithole campaigns. All unweighted claimed USD counts toward the tier threshold.
- Check the weekly cutoff before claiming. A claim counts in the weekly period in which it is recorded.
- Look for a campaign's `1×`, `2×`, or `5×` multiplier when you want to increase raffle entries; it does not increase your weekly reward-pool score.
