Rewards you claim during each raffle week determine your tier for that week. Your tier controls your share of the weekly USDC reward pool and the multiplier used to calculate your [raffle entries](/rabbithole/raffle#how-entries-are-calculated).

## The Leaderboard

Each raffle runs for four fixed, consecutive weeks. The weekly leaderboard starts fresh at the beginning of each week and ranks wallets by the USD value of rewards claimed during that week, before any campaign multiplier. It is not a rolling seven-day window.

The current week stays live until its cutoff, and claims made before the cutoff can move your rank and tier. When you are signed in, the app shows the weekly and monthly cutoffs. When the week closes, Rabbithole finalizes each wallet's claimed value, rank, and tier, and the next week begins.

The all-time leaderboard shows the USD value of rewards you have claimed since joining. It does not affect your weekly tier.

Each row shows the rank, the X handle or address, the number of campaigns the person is earning in, and the amount claimed. A check mark next to a name means a [verified account](/rabbithole/getting-started#verified-account).

## Tiers

Your tier is based on the USD value of rewards you claim during the current week, before any campaign multiplier:

| Tier | Weekly claimed USD |
| --- | ---: |
| Bronze | Under `$1` |
| Silver | `$1` or more |
| Gold | `$10` or more |
| Platinum | `$25` or more |
| Diamond | `$100` or more |

Everyone starts each week at Bronze. Your tier can only move upward as you claim more during that week, and claiming exactly `$1` is enough to reach Silver. At the weekly cutoff, your tier is finalized for that week and used for both the weekly reward calculation and that week's raffle-entry calculation.

<Note>
  Campaign multipliers do not affect your tier. Rabbithole uses your claimed USD before multipliers to determine the tier, then applies campaign multipliers only when calculating raffle entries.
</Note>

## The Weekly Reward Pool

Rabbithole currently distributes a **500 USDC pool each week**, for a total of **2,000 USDC across the four-week raffle**. Silver, Gold, Platinum, and Diamond wallets share the pool. Bronze has a weight of `0` and does not receive a share.

| Tier | Reward weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Bronze | `0` |
| Silver | `100` |
| Gold | `110` |
| Platinum | `125` |
| Diamond | `150` |

The weights make each claimed dollar count more at higher tiers. They are relative weights, not fixed USDC amounts or percentage returns.

```text
wallet score = weekly claimed USD × tier weight

wallet reward =
  $500 × wallet score / sum of all eligible wallet scores
```

For example, a Gold wallet that claims `$20` has a score of `20 × 110 = 2,200`. Its final reward is `$500 × 2,200` divided by the sum of every eligible wallet's score that week. Because that total changes as other people claim, any reward shown before the cutoff is a projection.

### Settlement and Publication

<Steps>
  <Step title="The Week Closes">
    Rabbithole finalizes each wallet's claimed USD, tier, score, and share of that week's pool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rewards Are Published">
    Each week's reward is added to a running total for the raffle. Rabbithole publishes the updated total, which includes every settled week so far.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You Claim">
    The app shows your unclaimed balance as claimable. Claim whenever you like. Each week's reward is paid once, so claiming early never costs you a later week.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Raffle Entries

The weekly reward pool ignores campaign multipliers. Raffle entries use them: each campaign's claimed USD is weighted by its multiplier, then your weekly tier multiplier is applied and the result is rounded down. See [Raffle](/rabbithole/raffle) for the full formula, the public snapshot, and how to verify the draw.

## Other Tier Benefits

From Gold upward, you can check in once a day to build a streak and earn a small reward. Open **Benefits** from the profile menu to see what your current tier gives you.

## How to Reach the Next Tier

- Claim at least `$1` during the week to reach Silver and become eligible for the weekly pool and the raffle.
- Claim across any Rabbithole campaigns. All claimed USD counts toward the tier threshold, whatever the campaign's multiplier.
- Check the weekly cutoff before claiming. A claim counts in the week in which it is recorded.
- Look for a campaign's `2×` or `5×` multiplier when you want more raffle entries. It does not increase your weekly reward-pool score.
