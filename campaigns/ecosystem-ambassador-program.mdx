Rabbithole selects a small cohort of ecosystem operators and connectors with genuine access to protocols, foundations, wallets, infrastructure teams, and other relevant organizations. Ambassadors introduce qualified supply-side partners and strengthen Rabbithole's relationships across onchain ecosystems. The program rewards verified commercial outcomes, not posts, impressions, clicks, or raw referrals.

<Note>
  This is not a referral program. **Users** who share campaigns with their invite link are covered in [Referrals](/rabbithole/referrals) in the Rabbithole section. **Partners** who bring depositors to a specific campaign are covered in [Referrals](/campaigns/referrals) in this section. Ambassadors introduce organizations that could run campaigns, and are paid only on verified commercial milestones.
</Note>

## Who the Program Is For

Strong candidates include:

- Ecosystem and foundation operators
- Protocol contributors
- Business-development and partnerships professionals
- Agency partners and consultants
- DeFi, wallet, and infrastructure connectors
- Trusted community leaders with genuine decision-maker access

Applications are public. Trusted nominations and curated invitations are also accepted. Every candidate goes through the same evidence-based vetting process.

## How the Program Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply or receive an invitation">
    Enter through the [public application](https://boost-xyz.notion.site/3b4a9c09443180dfb0e6d65131c805a7), a trusted nomination, or curated outreach.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete vetting">
    Rabbithole reviews identity, relevant experience, relationship evidence, commercial judgement, conflicts, and brand safety.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join the accepted cohort">
    Accept the program terms and receive an approved first action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit a target">
    Before any outreach, register the organization, the relevant contact, your relationship strength, the commercial relevance, and your proposed introduction path.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for approval">
    Rabbithole checks fit, duplicates, and the existing pipeline before approving outreach.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make a double-opt-in introduction">
    Both Rabbithole and the prospective partner agree to the introduction before it happens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rabbithole manages the commercial process">
    The team progresses the conversation from discovery through proposal, agreement, and delivery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify commercial milestones">
    Rabbithole records attribution, commercial value, evidence, cleared funds, and any eligible bounty.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The operating path is:

> Approved target → Double-opt-in introduction → Qualified meeting → Commercial opportunity → Verified commercial value → Eligible bounty

## Ambassador Responsibilities

As an ambassador, you are expected to:

- Identify organizations that genuinely fit Rabbithole.
- Register targets and obtain approval before outreach.
- Make warm, relevant introductions.
- Provide accurate context and relationship evidence.
- Use approved Rabbithole positioning.
- Protect confidential information and disclose conflicts.
- Follow attribution, conduct, and bounty rules.

<Warning>
  Ambassadors may not:

  - Negotiate pricing or contractual terms for Rabbithole.
  - Approve a partnership or make commercial commitments.
  - Claim to be a Rabbithole employee.
  - Promise liquidity, retention, revenue, rewards, or other outcomes.
  - Use yield, APY, farming, or guaranteed-return language.
  - Run spam, bulk outreach, or unapproved campaigns in Rabbithole's name.
</Warning>

Bounties are tied to **annual rewards distributed by an approved referred account**. Each threshold unlocks the stated bounty. Amounts are cumulative across achieved milestones for that qualifying account: USD 150,000+ in cumulative bounty. Terms apply.

### When a Bounty May Qualify

A bounty is considered only when all of the following hold:

- Rabbithole approved the target before outreach.
- You secured an approved double-opt-in introduction.
- The target was not already in Rabbithole's active pipeline.
- Attribution is recorded and remains valid.
- The commercial milestone and supporting evidence are verified.
- Rabbithole has received the relevant cleared underlying funds.
- Rabbithole gives final written approval for the liability and payment.

<Warning>
  No bounty is automatic or guaranteed.
</Warning>

### Attribution

- Attribution lasts **90 calendar days** after an approved qualified introduction.
- Credit normally belongs to the first ambassador who registered the target and secured the approved double-opt-in introduction.
- A dormant opportunity qualifies as a reactivation only with written approval.
- Rabbithole decides shared-credit cases and attribution disputes.

### When No Bounty Is Due

No bounty is due for:

- Unapproved, duplicate, or pre-existing active opportunities.
- Unpaid invoices or uncollected funds.
- Cancelled arrangements, refunds, or reversed transactions.
- Raw clicks, impressions, posts, signups, or community invites.
- Fraudulent activity, misleading submissions, or unauthorized side arrangements.
- Unapproved claims, spam, or misrepresentation.

## Selection and Activation

Rabbithole assesses:

- Ecosystem relevance and relationship quality.
- Access to suitable decision-makers.
- Evidence of previous introductions or partnership work.
- Commercial judgement and communication quality.
- Conflicts, brand safety, and expected support burden.

Acceptance is selective. You become an active ambassador only after accepting the approved terms, entering the operating roster, and receiving an approved first action.

## Program Measurement

Rabbithole evaluates the program using:

- Approved targets submitted.
- Double-opt-in introductions completed.
- Qualified meetings generated.
- Commercial opportunities created.
- Verified commercial value and received revenue.
- Evidence quality, support burden, and brand-safety record.

Follower counts, application volume, posts, and impressions are not primary success metrics.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is this a referral program?">
    No. It is a selective commercial ambassador program focused on qualified introductions and verified outcomes, not referral volume. For per-campaign partner referrals, see [Referrals](/campaigns/referrals).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can anyone apply?">
    Yes. Applications are public, but acceptance is selective. Nominated and directly invited candidates complete the same vetting process.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can ambassadors contact any organization?">
    No. Targets must be registered and approved before outreach using Rabbithole's name.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can ambassadors negotiate for Rabbithole?">
    No. Rabbithole handles pricing, proposals, contracts, and final commercial decisions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are bounties guaranteed?">
    No. Every bounty depends on approval, attribution, evidence, verified commercial value, cleared funds, and final payment approval.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are ambassadors paid for content or exposure?">
    Not under the first version of the Ecosystem Ambassador Program. Ecosystem exposure is part of the role but is not separately rewarded.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
